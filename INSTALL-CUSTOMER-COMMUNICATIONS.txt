MCGRIFF'S CUSTOMER COMMUNICATIONS UPDATE
========================================

This update adds:
- Signed checkout packet email with PDF attachment
- Customer signature in the PDF
- Pre-rental inspection details without photos
- Automatic 24-hour due reminder
- Automatic due-today reminder after 8:00 AM
- Automatic overdue notices at 1, 24, and 48 hours
- Automatic return thank-you email with return PDF
- Post-rental inspection details without photos
- Google Drive document archive
- Rental Email Center with resend buttons

IMPORTANT: UPDATE THE EXISTING APPS SCRIPT
------------------------------------------
1. Sign in to mcgriffsrental@gmail.com.
2. Open the Apps Script project currently deployed at the URL used by the employee app.
3. Replace the entire Code.gs file with the Code.gs included in this folder.
4. Click Save.
5. Run the setup() function once from the Apps Script editor.
6. Approve the requested Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, and spreadsheet permissions.
7. Click Deploy > Manage deployments.
8. Edit the existing web-app deployment.
9. Under Version, choose New version.
10. Confirm Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
11. Click Deploy.

Using a new version of the EXISTING deployment should keep the same web-app URL.
If Google gives you a different deployment URL, replace EMAIL_SERVICE_WEB_APP_URL near the top of app.js.

AUTOMATIC SCHEDULE
------------------
The Apps Script checks every 15 minutes.
- 24-hour reminder: approximately 24 hours before due
- Due-today reminder: after 8:00 AM on the due date, provided it is not already due
- First overdue notice: approximately 1 hour overdue
- Second overdue notice: approximately 24 hours overdue
- Third overdue notice: approximately 48 hours overdue

DOCUMENT ARCHIVE
----------------
Apps Script creates a Google Drive folder named:
McGriff's Rental Documents

Inside it, packets are organized by year and rental/customer.
Inspection photos are NOT included in customer PDFs or emails.
