MCGRIFF'S RENTAL MANAGER — MOBILE APP V6

WHAT THIS UPDATE FIXES

1. Phones and touch-screen tablets automatically open mobile.html.
   Add ?view=desktop to the URL when you intentionally want the full office portal.

2. Mobile checkout now creates the same signed contract record used by the desktop portal.
   The contract and signature will therefore appear later from the desktop Contracts/Rental screens.

3. Existing-customer contact information is copied into the rental.
   New-customer contact information is also copied into the rental.

4. When the customer has an email address, mobile checkout submits the signed contract email through the current McGriff's email Apps Script.

5. Checkout still saves successfully if email submission fails. The completion screen clearly reports the email status.

6. The top-right menu button opens the full desktop portal when needed.

INSTALLATION

Upload every file in this folder to the root of the mobile-redesign branch, replacing matching files.
Wait for GitHub Pages to update, then hard-refresh once.

TEST ON A PHONE OR TABLET

1. Open the normal employee portal URL. It should automatically open mobile.html.
2. Log in.
3. Choose Rent Equipment or tap a reservation pickup.
4. Select/create the customer and enter the due time.
5. Take all four inspection photos.
6. Complete fuel, hours, accessories, condition, and checklist.
7. Review the agreement and sign with a finger or stylus.
8. Complete checkout.
9. Confirm the equipment becomes Rented Out.
10. Confirm the signed contract appears in the desktop portal.
11. When an email is on file, confirm the completion screen says the contract was submitted.
12. Use Return Equipment to complete all four photos, inspection, signature, and check-in.

HOME-SCREEN INSTALL

Android/Chromebook: Chrome menu > Install app or Add to Home screen.
iPhone/iPad: Safari Share button > Add to Home Screen.
